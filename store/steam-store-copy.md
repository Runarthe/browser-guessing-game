## Early Access questionnaire  [DECIDE: only if launching in EA]

Valve's own guidance: do not promise dates or specific features, because
buyers should be judging the current state. These answers follow that.

**Why Early Access?**
```
Confetti is a party game, and party games are only really finished once a lot
of different groups have played them. The core is built and playable — what I
want now is far more people playing it than I can gather in a living room, so
the minigames that people actually replay get the attention, and the ones that
don't can be reworked or dropped.
```

**Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?**
```
Long enough to act on what players report rather than to a fixed date. I would
rather leave Early Access when the game is right than when a calendar says so.
```

**How is the full version planned to differ?**
```
The intention is to expand online play beyond a single wifi network, to keep
refining the existing minigames based on what players actually enjoy, and to
add more of them. Nothing here is a promise — buy it for what it does today.
```

**What is the current state of the Early Access version?**
```
Fully playable, not a demo. 19 minigames, 2 to 8 players, everyone joining
from their own phone on the same wifi, scoring across a full night, and
unlockable cosmetics. Everything described on this page works today.
```

**Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?**
```
The price may rise when the game leaves Early Access. Buying during Early
Access will always be the cheaper option.
```

**How are you planning on involving the Community?**
```
Through the Steam forums and reviews. The most useful thing anyone can tell me
is which minigames their group asked to play again, and which ones they
skipped — that feedback shapes what I work on next.
```

## Things to settle before publishing

- `[DECIDE]` **Price.** Party games at this scope typically sit low; whatever
  you pick, set Early Access below your intended 1.0 price so you can raise it.
- `[DECIDE]` **Early Access or a straight 1.0 release.**
- `[DECIDE]` **The same-wifi line.** It appears twice. It must be accurate at
  launch — vagueness here converts directly into refunds.
- `[DECIDE]` **Do you claim all 19 minigames?** Claiming 19 sets an
  expectation that all 19 are good. Cutting the weakest before launch and
  claiming a smaller, stronger number is often the better trade.
- Capsule art in Steam's exact sizes, 5+ screenshots, and a trailer. For a
  party game the trailer does most of the selling — real gameplay, real
  reactions, 30–60 seconds.
